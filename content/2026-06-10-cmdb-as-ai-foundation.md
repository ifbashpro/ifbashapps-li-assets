## Caption

Every AI recommendation is only as reliable as the data underneath it.

In ServiceNow environments, that data is your CMDB. If it's stale, incomplete, or manually maintained, your AI agents are working from a flawed map — and your change risk scores, root-cause analysis, and autonomous decisions will reflect that.

A mature, discovery-fed CMDB with accurate CI relationships is the single highest-leverage investment you can make before turning on Now Assist, AIOps, or any agentic workflow.

ifBash treats CMDB remediation as a first-phase deliverable — not an afterthought — because getting the foundation right is what makes everything else actually work.

Garbage in, garbage out. Even for AI.
