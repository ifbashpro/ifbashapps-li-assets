## Caption

Everyone says their AI agent 'resolves issues.' Very few can show you the workflow that proves it.

There's a real distinction worth making in 2026: a chatbot that surfaces a knowledge article is not the same as an agent that raises a change request, validates approvals, provisions access, and closes the ticket — autonomously.

ServiceNow's agentic layer connects Now Assist to live workflow orchestration. That means actions, not just answers. But it also means you need guardrails: approval gates, audit trails, rollback logic, and scope boundaries that your governance team can actually stand behind.

At ifBash, we help clients design agentic AI deployments that are genuinely autonomous where safe — and human-in-the-loop where it counts. The hype cycle is loud. The architecture is what matters.

What's your threshold for letting an AI agent take action without human approval? Worth discussing.
