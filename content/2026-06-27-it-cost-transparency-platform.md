## Caption

IT budgets are under pressure. But most IT leaders still can't answer: what does it cost to deliver this service?

ServiceNow IT Business Management changes that. By connecting financial data to your service portfolio and CMDB, it surfaces real cost-per-service metrics — not estimates, not spreadsheet models. Actual platform-driven transparency.

Illustratively, organisations using ITBM identify 20–30% of IT spend tied to low-value, redundant, or unowned activities. That's budget that can be reallocated to transformation initiatives — with the data to defend it to the CFO.

ifBash implements ITBM as part of a broader platform strategy — not a standalone finance module, but a connected capability that makes your entire ServiceNow investment more defensible.

If you can't show the board what IT costs and what it delivers, that conversation is coming. Be ready for it.
