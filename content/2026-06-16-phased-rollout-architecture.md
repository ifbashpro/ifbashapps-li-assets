## Caption

The biggest ServiceNow implementation risk isn't technical. It's trying to do everything at once.

Big-bang go-lives create big-bang problems: scope creep, user resistance, data quality gaps surfaced at the worst possible moment. The teams that get the most from ServiceNow start smaller, validate faster, and expand on a foundation that actually works.

ifBash's phased rollout architecture sequences capability deployment by business impact. Core ITSM and service catalogue go live in the first sprint. AIOps, automation layers, and self-service capabilities follow in structured phases — each building on validated data and adoption from the last.

Illustratively, this approach targets core ITSM live within 4 weeks and full production capability by day 73. Not because we rush — because we sequence.
