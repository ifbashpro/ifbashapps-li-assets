## Caption

How many items are in your ServiceNow Service Catalogue right now? How many were last requested in 2023?

Catalogue sprawl is one of the most common — and most expensive — platform problems we see. Hundreds of items, no clear owners, inconsistent fulfilment, and end users who've given up and gone back to emailing IT.

ServiceNow's catalogue governance framework gives every item an owner, a fulfilment SLA, a review cycle, and a retirement trigger. Combined with AI-powered search and a well-designed Employee Center front-end, a governed catalogue becomes a genuine self-service engine.

ifBash approaches catalogue design as a governance exercise first, a technology exercise second. Because the best catalogue in the world fails without accountability behind it.

Does your catalogue work for your users — or against them?
