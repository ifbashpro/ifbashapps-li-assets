## Caption

How many spreadsheets does your organisation use to track physical assets? If the answer is 'more than one,' you already know the problem.

ServiceNow Enterprise Asset Management (EAM) is purpose-built to consolidate IT hardware, OT equipment, and facilities assets onto one intelligent platform. That means a single source of truth for asset lifecycle — from procurement and deployment through maintenance scheduling to retirement and disposal.

What makes EAM genuinely powerful is its connection to the broader ServiceNow data model. Assets link to CIs, to services, to risk records. AI-driven maintenance forecasting replaces reactive break-fix cycles. Compliance reporting becomes a query, not a project.
