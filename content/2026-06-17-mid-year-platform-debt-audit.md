You're halfway through 2026. Do you know which parts of your ServiceNow platform are actually working?

Most enterprises accumulate silent technical debt on ServiceNow: workflows built for a process that changed, modules licensed but never adopted, automations that run but no longer align to current policy. It doesn't break anything visibly — it just slows everything down.

June is the natural audit window. H2 budgets are forming. Platform roadmaps are being refreshed. A structured usage and hygiene review right now tells you what to prune, what to accelerate, and where to invest next.
